Privacy Policy — Coin Vault

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Coin Vault is made by Bethel Ventures. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

The short version: nothing leaves your phone. Coin Vault has no servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. We do not collect, sell, share, or send your personal information anywhere — the app collects no personal data at all.

How the app works

  • You build a catalog of your coins, paper money, antiques, and other valuables right on your phone. Every item you add — its name, details, grade notes, and estimated value — stays on your device.
  • The app's reference guides (coin values, known errors and varieties, and grade guidance) are built into the app. Looking something up sends nothing anywhere — there is no server for it to go to.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your collection is stored in a private database on your phone only.
  • Photos you add to an item are copied into the app's private storage on your phone only. They are never uploaded, emailed, or transmitted anywhere.
  • When you delete an item, its photos are deleted with it. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Camera and photos

  • The app uses your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to one of your items. It never accesses your camera or photos on its own.
  • Enhanced photos are created on your device, from your photos, and the originals are kept untouched. Nothing about the process leaves your phone.

What the app does not do

  • It does not take payments or ask for bank, card, or account information.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, or messages.
  • It does not show ads and contains no advertising or tracking software.

Children: Coin Vault is made for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes: If a future version changes how the app handles information, this policy will be updated and the new version posted at the same address before the change takes effect.
